ABOUT ME

My name is Nyein Min Han. I am from Myanmar
studying in Japan at Tokyo Business Academy. My name is too long so just call me Ian. I
was born in 2004 January 2nd. I attended international school from pre-kinder garden
until junior high and transferred to a Burmese School for my senior high. I graduated and
started planning to go abroad because I was not satisfied with my country's education system. I
started watching Anime a few years before coming to Japan and started learning Japanese after
graduating. 1 year after I started, I was lucky enough to come to Japan in 2020 December. Drawing
is one of my hobbies and my favorite as well. I will be drawing in every free time. Because of that,
I was drawn into being an artist, but I didn't have the confidence to make a living out of it. This is why
I choose WEB Designing. Whenever I see a website, I feel really attracted to the animations and the cool
designs of the websites, which made me want to create cool websites and make others feel the same
way I did. I had never learned about programming or illustrations until I came to Tokyo Business Academy.
It was never easy to learn new things, but my passion for art and the mindset of challenging new things always helped me through.
